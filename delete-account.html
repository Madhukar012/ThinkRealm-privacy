ThinkRealm : Maze Island — Account Deletion Request

If you would like to delete your Maze Island account and all associated data, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

Step 1: Send an email to playpassionofficial@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request — Island Maze

Step 2: Include the email address associated with your Island Maze account (your Google account email).

Step 3: We will process your request within 30 days and confirm deletion by email.

What Data Gets Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Your Google account email address
  • Your game progress, maze completion records
  • Your coin balance and rewards history
  • Your leaderboard entries and ranking data
  • Your user profile and statistics

Data Retention

All personal data is deleted within 30 days of receiving your request. Anonymous, aggregated gameplay statistics that cannot be linked to your identity may be retained for service improvement purposes.
